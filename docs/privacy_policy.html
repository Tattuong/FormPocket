Privacy Policy – FormPocket

Last updated: October 2026

FormPocket keeps profiles, templates, coins, and shop unlocks on this phone. We do not create an account or upload your details to our servers.

Data stored on device

  • Profile fields, templates, and the optional app PIN
  • Coins, owned shop items, and the active look
  • Purchase tokens needed to restore Remove ads and FormPocket Pro

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – coin packs, Remove ads, and FormPocket Pro (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – GET https://api2.blwsmartware.net/N295.json to read the disable field (no personal data is sent)

We do not sell personal data. Contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
